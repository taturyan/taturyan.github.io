Gayane Taturyan

Postdoc · S2A, LTCI · Télécom Paris

Since 2025, I am a postdoctoral researcher at Télécom Paris, working with Charlotte Laclau and Stephan Clémençon. I am a member of the S2A team within the LTCI lab.

My main research interests are in machine learning, with a focus on constrained learning, particularly in the context of algorithmic fairness across various settings, including regression, classification, ranking, and recommender systems.
